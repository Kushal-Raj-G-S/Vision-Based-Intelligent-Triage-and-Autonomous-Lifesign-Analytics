Existing workLimitation / research gapHow VITAL addresses it
Classical rPPG (edge and transit systems) Tested mostly on one person in controlled scenes; little on breathing or skin tone Heart rate and breathing from one webcam, checked on several public datasets
Deep-learning rPPG (e.g. DeepPhys) Accuracy drops with head motion, occlusion and lighting; breathing barely validated Signal-quality, motion and lighting checks, plus an ML correction trained and tested on separate people
Wearables, telehealth and sensor-fusion reviews Wearables need compliance and raise privacy issues; fusion setups are hard to synchronise and calibrate Contactless: one ordinary webcam, no wearable or extra sensor
AI triage tools and clinical-impact reviews Few use contactless sensing; weak external validation, explainability and clinician trust Standard ESI acuity from a rule engine, with explanations and a mandatory manual-review flag for shock
Multi-agent LLM and RL systems (decision support, mental health, hospital resources) Text-only or simulated; not tested on live physiological signals; the LLM can invent numbers Agents work on real measured vitals; the rule engine, not the LLM, sets priority
